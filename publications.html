  1. CIKM 2026

    Bandit-Quota: Latency-Budgeted Multi-Retriever Selection via Contextual Thompson Sampling

    Priyank Jayraj, Poonam Goyal, Navneet Goyal

    Proceedings of the 35th ACM International Conference on Information and Knowledge Management, pp. 7256–7260.

    A training-free selector that picks a budgeted subset of dense retrievers under a wall-clock cap. Across seven BEIR collections it is 2.4x to 9.3x faster than a 10-arm ensemble, and matches or improves nDCG@10 on six of them.

  2. arXiv 2026

    Exact Unlearning After Data Curation

    Priyank Jayraj, Devanshu Sahoo, Uday Mittal

    arXiv:2610.33426.

    If earlier similar records block later ones, deleting a raw record can admit an example that was excluded. A bound on future deletions identifies which excluded records can still enter training, and the training summary can be repaired to match a rebuild.
