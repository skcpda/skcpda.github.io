Research

Three problems, each with a hard constraint. Long documents where flat retrieval clusters in one region. Training pipelines that drop near-duplicates and then have to forget a record exactly. Retriever ensembles that miss the latency budget if every expert runs. These are the recent papers on Google Scholar.

Long documents · retrieval

NavTree

Summary trees for long-document QA are usually credited to the summaries. NavTree keeps the tree and drops the language model at indexing: a deterministic balanced segment tree over chunks, walked from the retrieved leaves, emitting only raw chunks to the reader.

arXiv:2610.06902 · zero language-model calls at indexing

Paper

Unlearning · curation

Exact unlearning after data curation

If earlier similar records block later ones, deleting a raw record can admit an example that was excluded before. A limit on future deletions identifies which excluded records can still enter training, and the training summary can be repaired to match a rebuild.

arXiv:2610.33426 · 13,056 deletion-step checks on 1,001 AskUbuntu records

Paper

Latency · bandits

Bandit-Quota

A training-free, per-query selector that picks a budgeted subset of dense retrievers under a wall-clock cap. Linear Thompson sampling over a small query sketch, with a cached fusion fallback when the budget would otherwise slip.

CIKM 2026 · 2.4x to 9.3x faster than a 10-arm ensemble · nDCG@10 match or better on 6 of 7 BEIR datasets
